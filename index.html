About EPIC-EDGE

We are a performance-driven mechanical design and engineering service provider specialising in 3D modelling, drafting and detailed design. Our team has over 5 years of experience in CAD software and a strong background in the outsourcing and offshoring industry.

We turn concepts into precise technical drawings and models for many industries, and we meet tight deadlines while keeping costs sensible.

Our work extends to design for assembly and manufacturing, so client requirements arrive as production-ready designs. Based in Rajkot, Gujarat.
